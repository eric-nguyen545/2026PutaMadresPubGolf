How to Play

Hole-by-Hole Mechanics

⛳ Before You Tee Off

In total, there are nine holes. To keep things fair, and interesting, every player needs 4 beers, 4 liquor-based drinks, and 1 dealer's choice across the round. Each hole lists a par and a recommended pour, but that's exactly what it is: a recommendation, you can choose anything containing alcohol available at a bar. Look over the full list before you tee off and pick your combination accordingly.

Whatever combination you land on needs Eric's sign-off; his call is final, and if you don't agree with it, you can fuck right off.

One hole allows canned drinks only, so plan around it, and keep an eye out for hazards along the way. We're cart-path only today; have fun golfing!

1

One Gulp Per Hole

Every player takes one gulp of the hole's drink before moving on.

2

Assists After The Turn

Once we've passed the turn, teammates can drink for each other to help finish the hole - one gulp is still required.

3

Trust Your Caddie

Robert Weber carries the full rulebook and calls out anything course-specific before you leave each bar. Listen up.
