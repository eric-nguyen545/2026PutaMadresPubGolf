How to Play

Hole-by-Hole Mechanics

1

One Gulp Per Hole

Every player takes one gulp of the hole's drink before moving on.

2

Assists After The Turn

Once we've passed the turn, teammates can drink for each other to help finish the hole — one gulp is still required.

3

Trust Your Caddie

Robert Weber carries the full rulebook and calls out anything course-specific before you leave each bar. Listen up.
